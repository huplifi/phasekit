# Contextual restrictions on refrigerant cards

Reviewed 2026-09-25 against [Regulation (EU) 2024/573](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/oj), Article 13 and Annex IV, [Regulation (EU) 2024/590](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/590/oj), Articles 4–5 and 21, and the [Finnish environmental administration’s current guidance](https://www.ymparisto.fi/fi/luvat-ja-velvoitteet/f-kaasut-ja-otsonikerrosta-heikentavat-aineet). `restrictionsFor` provides dated **contextual notices**, never a refrigerant-wide legality verdict. Equipment type, rated capacity, installation site, refrigerant condition and special exemptions must be checked for an actual transaction or service job.

The restriction list is data in `packages/rulesets/eu-fi/src/restrictions.ts`. It distinguishes use for *maintenance/servicing* from *placing new equipment on the EU market*. Annex IV restrictions generally do not make existing installed equipment illegal to operate. The source has limited safety exceptions, and Commission implementing regulations can grant time-limited equipment exceptions. For example, [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/33](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/33/oj) allowed specified self-contained refrigeration equipment to be placed on the market until 30 June 2026 despite Annex IV point 4. [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2729](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2024/2729/oj) is another equipment-specific point 4 derogation. A notice therefore names the exact category and instructs the user to check applicable derogations.

Article 13(3) restricts GWP ≥2500 F-gas use for refrigeration servicing from 2025, with reclaimed/recycled existing-equipment exceptions only until 2030 and military, deep-freeze and authorised exemptions. Article 13(4) applies the 2026 GWP ≥2500 restriction to Annex I gas used in air-conditioning and heat-pump servicing; reclaimed/recycled existing-equipment exceptions run until 2032. The 2030 and 2032 exception expiries have separate timed notices, so a future change is visible before its date. Article 13(5) begins in 2032 for GWP ≥750 Annex I gas in stationary refrigeration excluding chillers; its reclaimed/recycled existing-equipment exceptions have no fixed end date in that paragraph, and military, deep-freeze and nuclear-plant cooling exceptions exist. The code’s notices expose these distinctions and do not claim that all servicing is prohibited.

Annex IV contains many product-specific prohibitions. The current data table covers refrigerant-card notices for domestic refrigerators/freezers, self-contained and other refrigeration, chillers, self-contained A/C and heat pumps, and the dated split-system transitions. It does **not** exhaust Annex IV’s mobile, fire-protection, electrical-switchgear and other product categories. A full equipment decision needs a separate tool with subtype, capacity and exemption inputs. The refrigerant card must not say that omitted categories are unrestricted.

For GWP-dependent notices, a pure gas requires a verified EU-annex 100-year GWP fact and basis; a blend requires a verified Annex VI whole-mixture GWP or verified component fractions and each component’s EU-annex GWP. Leak-check scoring remains separate: it uses the Annex I CO₂e subtotal and Annex II(1) mass subtotal. If regulatory GWP or provenance is missing, the card omits numeric-threshold notices and can still show category-wide F-gas notices. It must show the data coverage state, because absence of a notice is not evidence that there is no restriction.

ODS substances such as R22 remain findable as historical refrigerants. Regulation 2024/590 restricts their use and market supply; existing equipment may remain in operation, but Finland’s guidance says it may not be refilled after leaks or failure. Article 21 periodic checks remain a separate obligation. Specific ODS exceptions must be assessed case by case.

For a blend with a **verified** composition and a source-backed Annex I ODS component, the card shows the Article 4–5 ODS restriction notice. Historical R500/R502/R503/R509A currently have only partial CoolProp component evidence, so the card instead shows an explicit **ODS component indicated; verify composition and applicability** advisory. This is not a verified composition, a refrigerant-wide legality verdict, or a leak-check result. The leak-check engine still returns insufficient data for these partial blends. The advisory requires both a sourced blend component link and a component Annex I ODS classification backed by EU 2024/590; an unsourced or merely named component does not trigger it.

Before product release, a qualified refrigeration/regulatory reviewer must approve the blend GWP basis, equipment category wording, each Article 13 exception, current implementing derogations and the Finnish copy. Changes in EU law or national guidance need a new versioned ruleset, dated source review and regression vectors; notices do not update silently offline.
