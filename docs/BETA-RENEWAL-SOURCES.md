# Beta renewal: source decisions and open data limits

Checked 2026-10-02. This record supports BETA-01–04, 12, 20, 23 and 26–27. It distinguishes a directly sourced value, a stated physical inference, and a missing classification. It is not an installation-specific compliance opinion.

| Topic | Verified source and resulting decision | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature glide | The canonical data require temperature, absolute pressure, phase and method for a numeric nominal glide. A verified `0 K` at stated conditions is displayed **“Ei liukumaa”**. A pure substance's bubble and dew points coincide at fixed pressure by the definition of a one-component phase equilibrium; the app may display the same wording when the pure identity is verified, while explaining the inference. | An unknown blend glide is not zero. A nominal glide at one pressure is not a promise of the same number everywhere. |
| Density | The canonical data validator requires unit and complete phase/temperature/absolute-pressure/method conditions for a displayed density. A chemical name or CAS alone is insufficient. | The beta does not generate new density values from an unverified property model. A stored number without conditions is withheld from the fact display. |
| PED fluid group | [Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU, Article 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32014L0068) defines fluid groups 1 and 2 from applicable CLP hazard classes and equipment conditions. | PED fluid group is distinct from ASHRAE refrigerant safety group. Missing catalog values remain missing; no conversion from A1/A2L/A3 is valid. |
| Lower flammability limit and autoignition temperature | These are different material properties. The canonical `Fact` state distinguishes verified, unknown and not applicable; source conditions accompany a verified numeric value. [ASHRAE Standard 34 overview](https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/standards-and-guidelines/ashrae-refrigerant-designations) explains the safety-class framework. | “Ei syty” is not substituted for either absent number. A not-applicable marker for one property does not certify general non-flammability under all conditions. |
| R1243zf family | [US EPA, *Transitioning to Low-GWP Alternatives in Motor Vehicle Air Conditioning Systems*, footnote 3](https://www.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2015-07/documents/transitioning_to_low-gwp_alternatives_in_motor_vehicle_air_conditioning_systems.pdf) explicitly identifies HFO-1243zf as R-1243zf. Canonical `legal_family` is therefore HFO with the new `epa-hfo-1243zf-family` source. | This source does not assign an ASHRAE safety group or EU Annex status. Those remain independent. |
| R1243zf safety class | The accessible [ASHRAE designation list](https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/standards-and-guidelines/ashrae-refrigerant-designations) establishes the designation but did not expose a checked safety class. Published research gives inconsistent A2/A2L labels, so the beta retains **classification missing from dataset**. | Do not infer safety class from HFO family, molecular structure or a vendor list. Obtain a current authoritative class source before filling the field. |
| Statutory certificate | [Finlex, VNa 1063/2025 §9](https://www.finlex.fi/en/legislation/2025/1063) lists eleven minimum certificate items for stationary refrigeration, AC and heat-pump installation. Item 5 requires a pressure-test report **if** pressure-equipment law mandates it; items 6–8 name evacuation, tightness and test-run reports. [Environmental Protection Act amendment 754/2024, 162 a §](https://www.finlex.fi/api/media/statute/690448/mainPdf/main.pdf?timestamp=2024-12-04T22%3A00%3A00.000Z) establishes the installation-certificate duty. | The certificate is distinct from ordinary technical commissioning and continuing service journal. A text reference does not demonstrate an attached or delivered protocol. A typed name/checkbox is not a verified responsible-person signature. |
| Pressure test | [Directive 2014/68/EU Annex I 3.2.2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32014L0068) addresses pressure-containment proof testing in conformity assessment. [Tukes guidance on on-site pressure-equipment assemblies](https://tukes.fi/paikalla-rakennettujen-painelaitekokonaisuuksien-ja-painelaitteiden-asennuksen-turvallisuusvaatimukset) highlights the role of technical documents and assessment route. | The app cannot decide installation-specific applicability or test pressure from refrigerant and charge. The guided form records a documented basis or leaves the assessment unresolved. A tightness test is not silently relabelled as a pressure-strength test. |
| Leak-check interval | [Regulation (EU) 2024/573 Article 5](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/oj/eng) gives equipment categories, Annex I CO₂e and Annex II section 1 mass thresholds, hermetic exceptions and 12/6/3 month tiers with eligible detection-system extensions. The existing `evaluateCheck` engine already records decisive rule, reason codes, date, sources and dataset/ruleset versions. | Use the same engine for a report, require additional equipment facts explicitly and leave unsupported/insufficient results unresolved. A catalog GWP without legal basis cannot be silently substituted. [Regulation (EU) 2026/1444](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/1444/oj/eng) repeals legacy detailed leakage-check regulations, not the Article 5 frequency tiers. |
| Vacuum target | [VNa 1063/2025 §9](https://www.finlex.fi/en/legislation/2025/1063) requires an evacuation report but provides no universal vacuum target or hold duration. | The user's common target below 2.7 mbar may be used when an applicable manufacturer/site instruction supports it. The beta does not prefill it or infer acceptance from missing readings. |

Source and data changes are confined to `data/refrigerants.csv`, `data/sources.csv`, generated catalog artifacts, and the fact-status helper. No thermodynamic P–T or P–h grids were changed for the R1243zf family correction. The safety-class ambiguity remains a documented gap.
