# PhaseKit EU/FI leak-check decision record

Source review: 25 September 2026. This document defines expected outcomes **before** the calculation engine. It describes the periodic leak-check question only; servicing, repairs, operator records and product restrictions are separate duties. Regulatory approval by a qualified Finnish specialist remains a release gate.

## Primary sources and interpretation

- [Regulation (EU) 2024/573](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/oj), Articles 2–3, 5–6 and Annexes I, II(1), VI. Article 5(1) starts at **at least** 5 t CO₂e of Annex I gas or 1 kg of Annex II section 1 gas. Article 5(6) sets 12/6/3 months at 5/50/500 t CO₂e or 1/10/100 kg respectively; an installed leak detection system doubles these intervals. Article 6 separately requires a detector for the covered stationary refrigeration, air-conditioning, heat-pump and fire-protection equipment at 500 t CO₂e Annex I or 100 kg Annex II(1). The legal GWP values are the applicable 100-year values in the EU annexes; another assessment basis cannot silently substitute.
- Annex I section 1 HFC GWP100 values use the regulation's AR4 basis, while section 2 PFC GWP100 values use its AR6 basis. PhaseKit requires the verified family to match the corresponding basis; an unspecified family or a conflicting basis yields insufficient data instead of a threshold decision.
- [Finnish environmental administration guidance](https://www.ymparisto.fi/fi/luvat-ja-velvoitteet/f-kaasut-ja-otsonikerrosta-heikentavat-aineet), the section on regular leak checks (checked 2026-09-25), explicitly says to account for HFC and HFO **fractions** in mixtures. Thus PhaseKit sums `charge × mass fraction × EU Annex I GWP / 1000` for Annex I components, and `charge × mass fraction` for Annex II(1) components. It evaluates both branches and uses the shortest applicable interval. The Annex VI weighted whole-mixture GWP serves mixture GWP reporting, but cannot erase the separate Annex II mass threshold.
- Article 5(1): a labelled hermetically sealed unit is exempt when it holds **less than** 10 t CO₂e of Annex I gas **or** less than 2 kg Annex II(1) gas; a labelled hermetic unit installed in a residential building is exempt under 3 kg of fluorinated greenhouse gases. The mixed-gas `or` wording can create a conflicting result when only one branch is below its hermetic limit; PhaseKit returns `unsupported` in that case pending expert interpretation rather than assert an exemption. A label without actual hermetic sealing, or sealing without the label, does not qualify.
- Article 5(3),(5): road trailers/trucks are covered; other listed mobile air-conditioning/heat-pump equipment has a transition until **12 March 2027**. The broad `other_mobile` input cannot distinguish all listed equipment and returns `unsupported` after the transition. Ordinary motor-car A/C is outside this calculator’s equipment types.
- [Regulation (EU) 2024/590](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/590/oj), Article 21(3): Annex I ODS in covered stationary refrigeration, air conditioning, heat pumps and fire-protection systems require checks at **3/30/300 kg** for **12/6/3 months**. A labelled hermetic system under 6 kg is exempt at the first tier. Detector presence does not double those intervals. R22 follows this ODS rule, never the F-gas “natural/outside scope” result. The Finnish page confirms the same limits and warns ODS equipment cannot be refilled after leaks.
- [US EPA SNAP blend compositions](https://www.epa.gov/snap/compositions-refrigerant-blends) and [Honeywell R513A TDS](https://www.honeywell-refrigerants.com/europe/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Honeywell-Solstice-513A-TDS_EN.pdf) independently give R513A **56.0% R1234yf + 44.0% R134a by mass**. EU Annex I gives R134a GWP 1430. At 50 kg, Annex I is 22 kg × 1430 / 1000 = **31.46 t CO₂e**; Annex II(1) is 28 kg. The HFC branch is 12 months, HFO branch 6 months, so the resulting interval is **6 months** (12 with detector). Whole-mixture GWP and 50 kg total are not threshold shortcuts.

## Independent expected vectors

These expected outputs are source-derived inputs to the tests, not generated by the engine. `ε` denotes a positive amount sufficiently small to straddle the stated threshold. Equality belongs to the higher tier. A detector doubles only F-gas intervals and is separately mandatory when Article 6 applies.

| Case | Below / equal / above | Expected without detector | With detector |
|---|---|---|---|
| R134a, Annex I at 5 t CO₂e | below / equal / above | below threshold / 12 / 12 mo | below threshold / 24 / 24 mo |
| R134a, Annex I at 50 t CO₂e | below / equal / above | 12 / 6 / 6 mo | 24 / 12 / 12 mo |
| R134a, Annex I at 500 t CO₂e | below / equal / above | 6 / 3 / 3 mo, detector required at equality | 12 / 6 / 6 mo |
| R404A, all Annex I components, 5/50/500 t CO₂e | below / equal / above each | same tier sequence as R134a | doubled |
| R1234yf, Annex II(1), 1/10/100 kg | below / equal / above each | below/12, 12/6, 6/3 mo | below/24, 24/12, 12/6 mo |
| R513A, mixed Annex I and II(1), 50 kg | 31.46 t CO₂e and 28 kg | 6 mo, R1234yf branch decisive | 12 mo, R1234yf branch decisive |
| R744, R717, R290, R600a | any charge | outside F-gas/ODS periodic-check rule | same |
| R22, ODS Annex I, 3/30/300 kg | below / equal / above each | below/12, 12/6, 6/3 mo | same, no doubling |
| Labelled hermetic R134a | below / equal 10 t CO₂e | exempt / interval by F-gas tier | same |
| Labelled hermetic R1234yf | below / equal 2 kg | exempt / 12 mo | exempt / 24 mo |
| Labelled hermetic residential F-gas unit | below / equal 3 kg total F-gas | exempt / applicable interval | same |
| Labelled hermetic R22 | below / equal 6 kg | exempt / 12 mo | same |

For pure Annex I gases, the exact 5/50/500 t boundary often maps to a repeating decimal kg value, so direct score-tier tests assert exact equality; charge-input tests assert values on either side at explicit decimal precision. This avoids falsely claiming a finite kg string represents a repeating quotient exactly.

Charge, composition fraction and legal GWP lexemes are limited to **40 decimal digits each**. Legal arithmetic uses a 256-significant-digit Decimal context and at most 100 blend components; within these bounds, finite-decimal component products and sums are exact. A longer charge is rejected as `insufficient_data` (`INVALID_CHARGE`) rather than rounded into a legal threshold. Pressure conversion through psi is nonterminating in the reverse direction and is displayed at up to 40 significant digits; pressure values do not enter leak-check thresholds.

## Deliberate limits and release blockers

- A qualified EU/Finnish refrigeration-regulation expert must confirm the separate Annex I/II mixture treatment, the combined interval, and mixed-gas hermetic `or` cases. Source text and Finnish guidance strongly support fraction-based computation, but they do not supply a worked R513A example.
- Equipment subtype questions are insufficient for electrical switchgear, ORC installation date, broad mobile equipment, and fire protection’s alternative ISO/EN inspection regime. Those paths report `unsupported`, never “no check needed”.
- This ruleset applies from 2024-03-11. Historical decisions before that date need the former regulation and return `unsupported`. Rule/version snapshots must travel with saved results; a newer dataset must not recalculate old results silently.
- `outside_rule_scope` means only that the periodic obligation under these two EU regulations does not arise from the selected natural refrigerant. It is not a general maintenance or safety conclusion.

## R514A and missing-data diagnostics — 2026-09-25 update

Verified Annex II section 1 plus non-fluorinated mixtures now use the mass of the listed HFO component. This follows Article 5 of Regulation 2024/573 and the Finnish environment authority's instruction to consider HFC/HFO fractions. R514A is 74.7% R1336mzz(Z) and 25.3% trans-1,2-dichloroethylene. A 26 kg stationary charge contains 19.422 kg Annex II-1 refrigerant and requires a six-month interval, or twelve months with detection, absent an applicable exemption. The second component remains visible in the calculation breakdown.

The legal text was checked from the Official Journal copy hosted by FAOLEX: [2024/573](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eur228342.pdf), Annex II p.51, Annex VI p.59; [2024/590](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eur228346.pdf), Annexes I and II. R1130(E) is not dichloromethane or tetrachloroethene. Its legal mixture GWP uses the explicit Annex VI default zero for unlisted non-fluorinated constituents; this does not assert measured zero GWP or zero ODP. Evidence is retained in `data/staging/r514a-legal-root.json`.

Annex I mixtures with non-fluorinated constituents retain the interpretation gate pending review of the full mixture CO₂-equivalent treatment. Missing-data results now identify the affected refrigerant and missing composition, annex classification or applicable legal GWP; diagnostics are frozen in saved results and older backups remain valid.
