## Reported issues

1. **CO₂e tool-label alignment.** In the `kg ↔ CO₂e` tool row, the exchange icon appears vertically offset relative to the neighbouring unit text. Review the visual centre of the complete label, including the CO₂ subscript and the leading tool icon. Align the SVG and label consistently without losing correct chemical typography. Acceptance: the row reads as one visually centred label at desktop and phone sizes. Evidence: user screenshot `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.49.34.png`.

2. **Picker result-count spacing.** The `249 kylmäainetta` count sits too close to the search field, especially its focus outline. Give the count clear separation from the full focus ring while preserving the stable modal/search layout. Acceptance: no crowding or overlap in focused/unfocused states, including narrow screens. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.50.10.png`.
3. **Picker scroll-continuation cue.** The bottom of the results region cuts through the next row without a clear indication that more results follow. Proposed treatment: a subtle bottom fade/shadow, optionally with a small downward chevron, visible only when more content remains below. It must disappear at the bottom and when all results fit, must not cover the last row or block interaction, and must update after filtering or resizing. Preserve the complete scrollable list; do not reintroduce a Show more gate. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.50.45.png`.

4. **Saved records: hierarchy, rhythm and equipment wording.** Collapsed rows need a coherent disclosure icon/title row, a clearly readable calculation summary and separated, smaller date/equipment metadata. The disclosure triangle currently occupies its own line in the supplied example. Replace implementation-oriented “Current equipment” / “Current equipment link” with plain “Equipment” / an explicit change-equipment action. The current/original distinction exists because a saved calculation can be reassigned while preserving its original snapshot; retain that provenance in storage, but show the original association as secondary history only when useful (for example when it differs). Do not repeat the same LN25 name in three prominent places.
5. **Expanded records: result first, detail second.** Present the primary saved result with the same visual hierarchy and unit formatting as the originating calculator (for example a prominent `28.6 t CO₂e` result), followed by compact key inputs. Move equipment management out of the main result-reading path. Keep component breakdown, provenance, source references and version metadata available as clearly labelled secondary sections, with readable spacing and consistent type sizes. Avoid repeating the total in several equally prominent rows. Localise raw implementation values such as `reconciled` into meaningful user language; do not imply verification beyond what the stored status actually means. Preserve saved values and their source/model versions; never recalculate historical results from current data just to render them. Check the shared layout across different record types, phone/desktop widths, and print/image output where shared components are affected. Evidence for items 4–5: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.51.31.png` and `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.52.48.png`.

6. **Chart framing: one switch instead of tabs.** The owner questions the Full range / Fit cycle tab treatment and proposes a single “Sovita kiertoon” (“Fit to cycle”) switch. Recommended behaviour: on = fit the calculated cycle; off = show the full supported model range. This is a view setting for one chart, not navigation between content sections. Keep temperature/entropy/specific-volume guide visibility independent. When no valid cycle exists, disable the fit switch with a short explanation; do not suggest that fitting is active or possible. Preserve correct centring and bounds, keyboard operation and an accessible switch label/state. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.55.27.png`. Proposal recorded only; implementation awaits GO.

7. **Chart guide-label collisions with all layers enabled.** The supplied full-range charts show overlapping specific-volume labels on the liquid side and crowded entropy/temperature labels near cycle points on the vapour side. Clarify the chart with all T/s/v layers enabled; do not solve this by silently disabling a selected layer. Physical curves may intersect, but labels must remain distinguishable. Proposed approach: collision-aware label placement with consistent spacing from cycle markers, saturation boundaries, other labels and plot edges; a restrained text halo/background; and fewer simultaneous inline labels when space is limited. Where a label cannot fit, keep its value discoverable through an accessible touch/keyboard detail mechanism or an external keyed legend. Do not move or fabricate curve geometry to separate labels. Preserve the cycle as the primary visual layer and use line patterns as well as any colour distinction. Acceptance: inspect all-layer and individual-layer states in full-range/fit views, desktop/mobile sizes and frozen print/image exports; no overlapping guide labels or obscured state-point numbers, and no hover-only access to omitted values. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.56.05.png` and `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.56.11.png`. Await GO before implementation.

8. **Bidirectional P–T calculator: make the calculated result explicit.** Both pressure and temperature are editable, but the current small “Entered” / “Calculated” labels and inline equality do not make a successful calculation sufficiently visible. Proposed direction: retain both editable fields in stable positions and add a shared-style result card below them, replacing the existing small inline summary rather than adding another duplicate. Track the last edited quantity: after pressure entry, emphasise the calculated temperature with pressure/refrigerant context; after temperature entry, emphasise the calculated pressure with temperature/refrigerant context. Show a clear “Calculated temperature” / “Calculated pressure” label, a prominent value and unit, and an unambiguous directional relationship. Preserve pressure basis (absolute/gauge), units and existing bubble/dew distinctions for blends; do not reduce a supported temperature pair to an unexplained single value. Keep the card geometry stable as direction changes, avoid moving focus, and announce valid result updates accessibly without excessive typing announcements. Invalid, incomplete, unsupported or out-of-range input must not leave a stale successful result visible. Acceptance: edit either field and change units/pressure basis/refrigerant, including blends and invalid inputs, on phone and desktop; the input and derived result are distinguishable without colour alone. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.57.21.png`. Design proposal only, pending GO.

9. **Print action icon/text spacing.** In the leak-check result, the printer icon touches the “Tulosta / tallenna PDF” label. Give the icon and text a consistent gap and vertical alignment using the shared action style; retain a coherent clickable target. Check this print-action variant and other uses of the same component, including narrow screens and wrapping labels, rather than fixing only one visible instance with incidental whitespace. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.58.35.png`. Documentation only; await GO.

11. **Leak-check result card: refrigerant and charge context.** Show the refrigerant selected for the assessment and the assessed total charge directly in the result card as a compact secondary context row (for example `R134a · 120 kg`). Keep the required inspection interval/outcome as the primary content. For blends show the selected blend designation and total charge, not just the regulated component names or their partial mass. Use the inputs belonging to the displayed assessment so editing an unsubmitted form cannot relabel an older result with new values. Retain this context in corresponding saved/printed/shared output where applicable. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 13.59.31.png`. Proposal recorded; await GO.

12. **Leak-check save/print actions are separated and inconsistent.** At the bottom of the expanded calculation/source details, only Save (or “Calculation saved”) is visible. Read-only inspection confirms direct print/PDF is already rendered near the result card in `CheckResultView`, while Save is rendered after that entire view in `Check`; printing is not contingent on saving. This separation makes printing hard to find from the bottom and differs from the shared Save/Print pattern elsewhere. Proposed direction: group Save and Print/PDF in one clearly labelled, consistently styled result-action area close to the result and before long technical details, with related copy/share actions coherently organised. Saving must not disable or hide printing. Avoid unnecessary duplicated action bars; check discoverability both with details collapsed and expanded on phone/desktop. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 14.00.11.png` and current `apps/web/src/views/Check.tsx`. Documentation only; await GO.

13. **Adjacent leak-check switches run together.** The “Leak detection system in use” label touches the following “Hermetically sealed” switch. Preserve the spacing within each switch/label pair and consistent alignment, including multiline Finnish/English labels and touch targets. Audit other uses of the same switch group for this spacing defect. Evidence: `Screenshot 2026-09-26 at 14.00.35.png`. Documentation only; await GO.

14. **Leak-check printout as a readable equipment/folder reference.** The owner wants a more visual printed document that can be attached to equipment or filed and understood at a glance. Reuse the on-screen result-card hierarchy in a print-appropriate layout: prominent assessment outcome and inspection interval; equipment/site identity when available; selected refrigerant and total charge. Do not present these as general service/maintenance dates: this calculator assesses leak-check requirements, not a complete maintenance programme. When no prior date or no supported schedule exists, show that state explicitly rather than inventing a due date. Keep assumptions, assessment date, sources/rule versions and limits available below the practical summary. Preserve frozen record dates/results for saved-report printing. Acceptance: the first printed page is useful without the app, readable in monochrome and at normal print size, does not split the result/date card across pages, and clearly identifies which equipment/charge the assessment concerns. Verify direct and saved-record printing, with/without a previous inspection date, and unsupported/exempt outcomes. User feedback at approximately 14:00 on 26 September 2026; no new screenshot supplied for this item. Documentation only; await GO.
