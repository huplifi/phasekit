# EU 2024/590 Annex II review (2026-09-25)

The [official Regulation (EU) 2024/590](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R0590), Annex II, lists:

| Canonical ID | Annex II substance | Published ODP | Canonical handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `r13i1` | CF3I, trifluoroiodomethane | 0.01–0.02 | Leave numeric ODP unknown because the annex reports a range. Leave legal class unsupported pending an `ODS-II` class and rule review. |
| `r40` | CH3Cl, chloromethane/methyl chloride | 0.02 | BITZER already independently reports numeric ODP 0.02. Leave legal class unsupported pending an `ODS-II` class and rule review. |

Annex II is specifically for ozone-depleting substances **not controlled under the Montreal Protocol**. It must not be collapsed into the existing `ODS-I` value or `none`. Neither entry is evidence for EU 2024/573 Annex I/II fluorinated greenhouse gas status or an EU 2024/573 legal GWP. Once the legal model supports this distinction, import the class and exact fact source in a separate reviewed change.
